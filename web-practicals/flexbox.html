Flex item properties in use

  • flex-grow: cards and footer groups can use available free space.
  • flex-shrink and flex-basis: the brand and CTA reserve their intrinsic space; cards start from a responsive basis.
  • flex-wrap, justify-content, and align-items: navigation, actions, and footer groups adapt to their container.
  • align-self: the footer’s social links align to the end of their flex line on wide screens.
